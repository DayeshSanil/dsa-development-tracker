Rules to protect your progress

When a problem traps you

  1. 0–5 min: restate constraints and example.
  2. 5–15 min: write brute force.
  3. 15–25 min: test patterns.
  4. At 25–30 min: use one hint/editorial.
  5. Close it and implement from memory; mark H if you needed help.
  6. At the topic deadline, mark Y/R and move on.

When college interrupts

  • Do not restart Week 1 after missing days.
  • During exams, pause new topics; rest if you need to.
  • After exams: one familiar problem, then restart current week at half workload.
  • One main project only. DSA and development continue in parallel.
  • Green questions get quick recall; prioritise Yellow/Red for deeper revision.
